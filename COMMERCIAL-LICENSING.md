The PolyForm Noncommercial License does not grant commercial-use rights. The separate [Corevanta Commercial Evaluation License](./EVALUATION-LICENSE.md) allows prospective commercial customers to evaluate the official compiled GitHub installer internally for up to 30 days. Production use, billable services, customer deliverables, and ongoing business use require a purchased Corevanta commercial license.

- Duration: **30 consecutive calendar days** from first use
- Scope: the official compiled installer on one computer
- Permitted: internal evaluation using internally authorized copies of the evaluator's own project, backup, and configuration files
- Not permitted: production use, billable work, customer deliverables, redistribution, or continued commercial use after the evaluation period

See the [complete evaluation terms](./EVALUATION-LICENSE.md). Contact [customerservice@corevantallc.com](mailto:customerservice@corevantallc.com) if more time or a broader evaluation is needed.

Each additional computer requires another license unless Corevanta LLC provides different terms in writing. Redistribution, resale, sublicensing, and offering Corevanta as a hosted or managed service are not included in the standard license.

For multiple computers, site licensing, integration, consulting, or redistribution rights, contact [customerservice@corevantallc.com](mailto:customerservice@corevantallc.com).
